Understat Players Dataset

Comprehensive football player statistics from Understat.com covering Europe's top 6 leagues (2014-2026). Features ~37,000 player-season combinations with advanced metrics including xG, assists, shots, and playing time. Daily updates for current season.

  • 10,700+ unique players tracked
  • EPL, La Liga, Bundesliga, Serie A, Ligue 1, RFPL
  • CSV and Parquet formats available

Team-level football statistics from Understat.com for Europe's top 6 leagues (2014-2026). Contains 1,320 team-season combinations across 140 clubs with advanced metrics including expected goals, match results, and pressing statistics.

Comprehensive match results dataset from Football-Data.co.uk covering Europe's top 5 leagues from 2000/01 to 2024/25. Features 95,000+ matches across 200 clubs with detailed statistics including goals, shots, corners, cards, and match outcomes.

About

Vibe Data Science focuses on creating high-quality, accessible football analytics datasets and tools. Projects emphasize comprehensive coverage, regular updates, and ease of use for data scientists, researchers, and football analysts.

All datasets are sourced from leading football analytics platforms and are maintained with daily updates for current seasons. Available in multiple formats (CSV, Parquet) for optimal performance and compatibility.
